📚 Your texts

Paste your text

Paste the text you want to learn. Punctuation is optional. If it has character names, only your character’s lines get used. Each text you load is saved in 📚, so you can switch between them.

If it doesn’t find the characters, type their names here, separated by commas, and load again.

Who’s speaking

Tap a name to leave it out if it isn’t really a character.

📦 Back up or move your texts

Your texts are saved on this phone twice: once in this copy of Text Runs and once in a backup copy it keeps on its own. To keep a backup somewhere else, like iCloud, or to move your texts to another copy of Text Runs, save a backup file here and add it there.

Adding never replaces anything. Texts you already have are skipped.

Check the lines

Tap a sentence to switch it between spoken and stage direction. Use the name menu to change who says a line. ▶︎ starts rehearsing from that line.
